## Backend facts and rulings plan 4 must respect

- **`Transfer` returns `true` once the transfer is under way** and finishes in the background; failure arrives as `TransferFailed`. The caller is usually the active device, which must keep answering on the same connection.
- **`PlayNext` and `AddToQueue` carry both `ItemID` (made by the sender) and `MediaFileID`.** An edit naming a gone item is a no-op; every applied edit raises `QueueRevision` by one and is pushed as a delta carrying the edit's fields (`ResponseQueueDelta`). Deltas can arrive out of revision order across devices: a client that sees a gap asks for `RequestSnapshot`. Applying the same `QueueEdit` through `LiveQueue.Apply` on a copy gives the server's result.
- **Only the active device may send** `ReplaceSource`, `RegenerateSource`, `InsertSourceItem`, `RemoveSourceItem`, `Advance`, `Previous` and state reports; others get a `HubException`. A state report from a device that is not active is ignored. `StateRevision` must rise for each report; a claim resets it.
- **Reconnect rule:** a claim is refused only while another online device is active and playing; a refused device must pause and become a controller. A claim's queue replaces the session's (its revision rises). The transfer target's claim is always accepted.
- Removing the playing item from the queue means skip (the active device advances at once, the item never enters history); a media file or playlist entry deleted from the library (`RemoveSourceItem`) lets the playing one finish first. `Advance(Item?)` and `Previous(Item)` name the item now playing and never error. These rules are in the previous handoff and the spec.
- Idle teardown is 10 minutes (`IdleTeardownMinutes` is a double so tests use 0.02); `SessionEnded` means every device keeps its local queue copy.
- A claim does not check media ownership (edits do); the claim's lists must not hold nulls (the server rejects them with a `HubException`).
- `ConfigRemote` defaults: grace 10 s, transfer timeout 5 s, history 50, queue 20000, message size 4 MB.
